## E3: CSV import on generated malformed files

- Rows that must be rejected and were **reported**: 8078/8078 (1.0000, Wilson 95% [0.9995, 1.0000]).
- Rows that must be rejected but were **imported or dropped silently**: 0/8078 (0.0000, Wilson 95% [0.0000, 0.0005])  (accepted invalid 0, accepted with changed values 0, lost 0).
- Harmless variants imported **with a changed or missing value**: 0/23182 (0.0000, Wilson 95% [0.0000, 0.0002]).
- Harmless variants **rejected** (false rejections, safe but annoying): 0/23182 (0.0000, Wilson 95% [0.0000, 0.0002]).

No operator produced a silent failure or a false rejection.
